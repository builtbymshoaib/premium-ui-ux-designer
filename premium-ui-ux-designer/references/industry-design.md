# Module 15 — Industry-specific design

## Principles
Match visual language and content to customer intent rather than recycling a single template.

## Implementation workflow
Portfolio: projects and evidence; agency: services and outcomes; restaurant: menu, hours, location and ordering; real estate: filters, property details and inquiry; ecommerce: product discovery, price, shipping and checkout; SaaS: product value, onboarding and usable dashboard. Verify industry-specific claims and requirements.

## Practical example
A restaurant site uses real food photography and readable menus; a property site emphasizes trustworthy listing data rather than dramatic motion.

## Acceptance criteria
User can complete the top industry task in a clear path; no invented business details or unlicensed assets.

## Review checklist
- [ ] Inspect existing implementation and preserve working behavior.
- [ ] Apply the implementation workflow to the actual content and constraints.
- [ ] Verify the acceptance criteria with rendered evidence where possible.
- [ ] Record exceptions, risks and untested conditions.
